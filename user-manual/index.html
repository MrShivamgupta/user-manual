One Click Yatra — User Manual

A complete walkthrough of every area of the platform: the public marketing website, the internal Admin/Staff CRM, the self-service Customer Portal, and the Vendor Portal. Each section below explains what the screen is for and the steps to do the most common tasks on it, with a real screenshot taken from the running application.

One Click Yatra has four distinct areas, each with its own audience and its own login (or no login at all, for the public site):

  • Public website — anyone on the internet: browse destinations and packages, send an enquiry, view a quotation your travel agent shared with you.
  • Admin / Staff CRM (/admin/...) — internal staff (SuperAdmin, Travel Agent, Operations, Finance) manage leads, quotations, bookings, vendors, payments and reports.
  • Customer Portal (/portal/...) — a registered customer's self-service area: their own bookings, quotations, payments and invoices.
  • Vendor Portal (/vendor-portal/...) — a hotel/transport/activity vendor's self-service area: their own rates, payments and invoice submissions.

Public Website

The public site is what every visitor sees before logging in. No account is required to browse destinations, search packages, convert prices to their own currency, or send an enquiry.

Home page

The landing page: a destination/date/traveler search bar, popular destinations, featured packages, a trust section, and real customer testimonials.

  1. Fill in a destination, travel dates and number of travelers in the hero search bar, then click Search to jump to matching packages.
  2. Click View all above "Popular Destinations" or "Featured Packages" to see the full lists.
  3. Scroll down to see why customers choose One Click Yatra and read real traveler feedback.

Browsing destinations

The destinations list shows every published destination as a card, plus an "at a glance" world map that groups destinations by country.

  1. Type in the search box to filter destinations by name.
  2. On the map, click a numbered green badge to fly out dashed lines to every destination inside that country (click the same badge again to collapse them) — this also filters the grid above to that country.
  3. Click any destination card to open its detail page, which shows a real street map pin for its exact location plus a photo gallery and package suggestions.

Searching & viewing packages

Search all published tour packages by destination, duration or price, then open one to see its full itinerary, inclusions/exclusions and pricing.

  1. Use the filters at the top of the search page to narrow by destination, category or budget.
  2. Click a package card to view full day-by-day details, then use Enquire Now to ask about that specific package.

Sending an enquiry

Anyone can submit a travel enquiry without an account. It lands directly in the Admin CRM's Enquiries queue for staff to follow up on.

  1. Fill in your name, phone/email and a short message describing your trip.
  2. Submit — a staff member will see it immediately under Admin → Enquiries.

Viewing a shared quotation

When a staff member sends a quotation, the customer receives a secure link (no login needed) showing every priced package option side by side.

  1. Open the link shared by your travel agent (by email or WhatsApp).
  2. Compare the options' price breakdowns, then click Approve a Package to accept one, or Decline.
  3. Use Download as PDF to save a copy of the quotation.

Currency converter

Every listed price is in INR by default. Visitors browsing from abroad can switch the currency shown next to each price using the switcher in the header.

  1. Open the currency dropdown in the top-right of any public page (next to Login/Register).
  2. Pick a currency (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD, AED or JPY) — every price on the site now shows an "≈ converted amount" next to the original INR price, using live exchange rates.

Login & registration

Customers and staff both sign in from the same login screen; new customers can self-register for a Customer Portal account.

After logging in, you're taken straight to your own dashboard: staff go to the Admin CRM, customers go to the Customer Portal, and vendors go to the Vendor Portal.

Admin / Staff CRM

The internal back office at /admin. What a staff member can see and do here depends on their role (SuperAdmin, Travel Agent, Operations, Finance) — every menu item and action button is permission-gated, so a user simply won't see sections they don't have access to.

Dashboard

The first screen after logging in: today's key numbers, revenue and conversion charts, and quick-action shortcuts for the most common daily tasks.

  • Use the + Quick Action button (or the colored tiles below the KPIs) to jump straight into creating a lead, quotation, booking or customer.
  • Overdue follow-up and expiring-quotation banners at the top link straight to the records that need attention.

Leads

Every prospective customer starts as a Lead — captured manually, from a public enquiry, or from WhatsApp.

  1. Click + New Lead on the list page, fill in the customer's contact details, budget and travel interest, and save.
  2. Open a lead to update its status as it progresses (New → Contacted → Quotation Sent → Negotiation → Confirmed/Lost), log follow-up notes, or assign it to another agent.
  3. From the lead detail page, click Create Quotation to start pricing options for that customer.

Follow-ups

A unified queue of every scheduled follow-up call/message across all leads, so no prospect is forgotten.

  1. Mark a follow-up as done once you've made the call, or reschedule it to a later date.

Quotations

Build one or more priced package options for a lead, then send a secure link the customer can review and approve without logging in.

  1. Open a quotation and click + Add Option to add a priced package (duration, hotel category, price per person, and an itemized cost breakdown).
  2. Mark your best option as Recommended — it's highlighted for the customer.
  3. Click Generate Share Link to get the secure customer-facing URL, or Download PDF for an offline copy.

Bookings

Once a customer approves a quotation (or staff creates one directly), it becomes a Booking that moves through a fixed status pipeline to Confirmed and beyond.

  1. Add every traveler under Passengers (name, age, ID proof) — the lead passenger is flagged.
  2. Add optional extras (airport transfer, travel insurance, etc.) under Add-Ons.
  3. Move the booking through its status: Draft → Quoted → Pending Payment → Confirmed. Once Confirmed, a travel voucher can be generated for the customer.

Customers

Every person who has booked or registered becomes a Customer record, with their full booking and payment history in one place.

Enquiries

Every enquiry submitted on the public website lands here for a staff member to triage and convert into a lead.

  1. Change an enquiry's status as you work it (New → In Progress → Converted/Closed).
  2. Use Convert to Lead once you're ready to start pricing options for them.

Vendors

Hotels, transport operators and activity providers your agency works with. Each vendor can optionally be given their own login to the Vendor Portal.

  1. Click + New Vendor, fill in their business and contact details, and save.
  2. Under the vendor's Rates tab, add each service they offer with its price.
  3. Record payments made to the vendor under the Payments tab as they fall due.
A vendor only gets portal access once a staff member links a user account to their vendor record.

Destinations & Master Data

The catalog data that powers the public site: destinations (with map coordinates), plus shared lookup lists like package categories and travel seasons.

  1. When adding or editing a destination, set its Latitude/Longitude fields so it appears correctly pinned on the public destination map.

Tour Packages

The pre-built packages customers browse and book on the public site — itinerary, pricing, gallery and publish status.

  1. Set a package's status to Published once it's ready to appear on the public site.

Payments & Invoices

Every payment collected against a booking, and every invoice generated for a customer.

Feedback Manager

Customer feedback submitted after a trip. High-rated feedback with a comment can be featured as a public testimonial on the Home page.

Reports & Analytics

Pre-built reports for sales, revenue and team performance, exportable for further analysis.

Users & Roles

Manage staff accounts and what each role is allowed to do, via a fine-grained permission matrix.

Only a SuperAdmin can access this section.

Audit Logs

A read-only trail of who changed what and when, across the whole CRM — useful for troubleshooting and compliance.

Settings

Agency-wide configuration: your company's branding (used on every PDF), the email templates sent to customers, and the status of connected integrations.

  1. Update your agency's name, logo and contact details under Agency Profile — this branding automatically appears on every PDF (invoices, quotations, vouchers).

Customer Portal

Once a customer registers or is booked by staff, they can log in at /portal to self-serve: track their bookings, review quotations, and manage payments without calling the agency.

Dashboard

A quick summary of the customer's own bookings and account, with shortcuts to every section of the portal.

My Bookings

Every trip the customer has booked, with full trip details, passengers, add-ons, and a downloadable travel voucher once confirmed.

  1. Open a confirmed booking and click Download Voucher to get the travel voucher PDF for check-in.
  2. Upload any requested documents (ID proof, visa, etc.) directly from the booking detail page.

My Quotations

Quotations a travel agent has sent, viewable here in addition to the emailed link — approve or decline directly from the portal.

Payments & Invoices

A full record of what's been paid and what invoices have been issued for each booking.

My Profile

Update contact details and password.

Vendor Portal

A vendor (hotel, transport operator, activity provider) that has been linked to a login by staff can sign in at /vendor-portal to manage their own rates, see booking requests relevant to them, and submit invoices for payment.

Dashboard

A quick summary of the vendor's own profile and shortcuts to every section.

Booking Requests

Bookings relevant to this vendor's destination(s), so they know what's coming up.

My Rates

The vendor's own published price list for the services they offer (room rates, transfer prices, etc.) — kept in sync with what staff see on the Admin side.

Payments & Invoices

Track what the agency owes and has paid, and submit new invoices for work completed.

  1. Click + Submit Invoice, attach the invoice file, enter the amount, and submit it for the agency's finance team to review.

My Profile

Update the vendor's own business contact details.
